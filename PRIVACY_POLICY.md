# PRIVACY_POLICY.md

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2026

Move Your Body ("the App") is an open-source fitness education application developed under the **AOSSIE** organization.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and used when using the application.

---

# Information We Collect

The application may store the following information to personalize workout recommendations:

- Name (optional)
- Age
- Height
- Weight
- Fitness goals
- Fitness level
- Workout intensity preference
- Target body regions
- Health conditions and injuries
- Workout history
- Progress information
- Custom onboarding text

---

# How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used only for providing application functionality, including:

- Personalized workout recommendations
- On-device semantic matching
- Progress tracking
- Workout history
- Reducing exercise repetition
- Improving future recommendations

---

# Local Data Storage

User information is stored locally on the device using SQLite.

The application does **not** upload this information to servers operated by the developers.

---

# On-Device Machine Learning

Move Your Body performs semantic matching using an ONNX version of MiniLM running directly on the device.

Your custom inputs remain on your device during processing.

---

# Internet Access

Some optional features may require an internet connection, including:

- Downloading exercise media
- Optional AI integrations provided by the user

Internet connectivity is **not** used to collect or sell personal information.

---

# Permissions

Depending on enabled features, the application may request:

- Internet
- Notifications
- Microphone (voice control feature)

Permissions are requested only when required.

---

# Third-Party Services

The application:

- Does not display advertisements.
- Does not sell personal information.
- Does not share personal information with third parties.

Future versions may allow users to provide their own AI API keys.

If enabled, requests are sent directly to the selected AI provider according to that provider's privacy policy.

---

# Data Sharing

Move Your Body does not sell, rent, or share personal information.

All user information remains on the user's device unless optional third-party integrations are used.

---

# Open Source

Move Your Body is an open-source project maintained by AOSSIE.

Only the source code is publicly available.

No personal user information is stored in the repository.

---

# Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children.

Parents or guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

---

# Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect locally stored information.

However, no storage method can guarantee absolute security.

---

# Your Choices

Users may remove their data at any time by:

- Clearing the application's storage
- Uninstalling the application

---

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Updates will be published in this file.
